## Introduction

In an era of information overload, we are dedicated to providing you with a pure and secure space for financial tracking. Piggy Bank eliminates cumbersome cloud synchronization and ad interruptions, returning to the essence of financial management: recording and planning. All data is strictly stored locally on your device, ensuring that every financial transaction is accurate and absolutely private.

## Key Features

- **Security & Privacy**: Adopts a pure local file storage mechanism and processing, requiring no internet permissions and eliminating the risk of data leakage. Your financial data remains entirely in your hands.
- **Multi-Target & Progress Management**: Supports both "Cumulative" and "Target" modes for piggy banks, intuitively displaying deposit progress and achievement percentages.
- **Custom Gesture Interaction**: Supports quick deposits and withdrawals via card swiping, and allows highly customizable left/right swipe shortcut commands in the settings.
- **Internationalization Support**: Features a built-in seamless bilingual switching mechanism and provides a first-launch language onboarding flow.
- **Bills**: Provides a date-folded transaction view with a layout optimized for mobile devices, giving you a clear overview of your income and expenses.
